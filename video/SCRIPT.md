# Selly — demo video script (English)

Adapted from @abdou's script (« Selly — script de la vidéo démo », Nov 19, 2026): same story, same ten shots,
in English, told through **Sokhna**, who runs **Maison Sokhna**, an atelier and craft shop in Sacré-Cœur, Dakar.

- **Length:** 3:00 for the main cut, 1:30 for the short cut (end of this file).
- **The line to remember:** "Your shop, your assistant, your money: straight into your wallet."
- **Three proofs, shown, not said:** the assistant answers from the real stock; the QR turns into a check live;
  the money lands in Sokhna's wallet, not Selly's.
- **Voice:** warm, short sentences, market words ("Available?", "How much?"). No crypto words before 1:46.
  Two Wolof touches where they ring true: "Nanga def" (how are you), "Jërëjëf" (thank you).
- **Music:** chosen by the voice-over artist. It cuts dead at the check mark (1:59) and comes back at 2:01.
- **Amounts, the same everywhere:** white sneakers 20,000 FCFA + delivery to Mermoz 1,000 = **21,000 FCFA**.

About 400 words of voice-over: a calm pace over 3 minutes. Time codes are where each line **starts**; the video is
cut to the recorded voice afterwards, so small differences are fine.

---

## The script, shot by shot

### 1 · 0:00–0:12 · The hook

**On screen:** close-up of a phone at night, 11 pm. Messages pile up: "Available?", "How much?", "Do you have it
in 42?", "Still open?". Selly (the mascot) peeks in from the edge, worried.
**Voice-over:**

> "Available? How much? Do you have it in 42?
> For Sokhna, who sells clothes and crafts in Dakar, the day never ends."

### 2 · 0:12–0:28 · The problem

**On screen:** split screen: a stock notebook full of crossings-out; a mobile-money screenshot to check; a message
"I'll pay on delivery". Each panel drops in with a thud.
**Voice-over:**

> "She answers the same questions all day long. She counts her stock by hand.
> And she chases every payment, one transfer after another."

### 3 · 0:28–0:42 · Selly

**On screen:** Selly's home page: "Your shop. Your assistant. More sales." rolling in letter by letter, the
products floating, the S stamp turning. Three words pop in under it: Shop · Assistant · Payments.
**Voice-over:**

> "Selly gives every seller three things: a shop online, an assistant that sells for her,
> and payments that land straight in her wallet."

### 4 · 0:42–1:02 · A product from one photo

**On screen:** Sokhna's dashboard → "Add from a photo". She snaps white sneakers and types: "White sneakers,
20,000. Sizes 40 to 44, two pairs each." The product card fills itself in, field by field. One tap: confirmed.
**Voice-over:**

> "To put something on sale, Sokhna takes a photo and writes the way she talks.
> The assistant builds the product: the price, the sizes, the stock. One tap to confirm."

### 5 · 1:02–1:14 · Share

**On screen:** she copies her shop link and posts it as a WhatsApp status. The link flies across the screen and
opens Maison Sokhna on a phone.
**Voice-over:**

> "Then she shares her link, where her customers already are: her status, her groups."

### 6 · 1:14–1:46 · The customer asks

**On screen:** Fatou opens the shop, taps "Ask Selly" on the sneakers, and types:

- Fatou: "Nanga def! Do you have these in 42?"
- Selly: "Mangi fi, jërëjëf! Yes, the white sneakers are in stock in size 42: two pairs left, 20,000 FCFA."
- Fatou: "Delivered to Mermoz, how much?"
- Selly: "Delivery to Mermoz is 1,000 FCFA, so 21,000 FCFA in all. Your order is ready: pay with the button below." + the order card.

Little "live stock" and "delivery" tags glow on the answer as it lands.
**Voice-over:**

> "Fatou has a question. She asks Selly, any time of day.
> The assistant knows the stock live, works out the delivery, and prepares the order.
> Sokhna? She's asleep."

### 7 · 1:46–2:12 · The payment

**On screen:** the payment page: 21,000 FCFA, the amount in sats, the QR, the countdown. Fatou taps "Open my
wallet" and approves. **Music cuts.** The QR turns into a check mark, confetti: "Thank you, it's paid."
**Voice-over:**

> "Fatou pays in bitcoin, on the Lightning network. A few seconds.
> _[one full second of silence on the check mark]_
> Paid."

### 8 · 2:12–2:32 · At Sokhna's

**On screen:** her phone buzzes. Dashboard: the order "Paid, to ship"; then the Wallet page, the balance counting
up.
**Voice-over:**

> "When she wakes up, Sokhna finds the order paid, ready to ship.
> And the money is already in her wallet. Not with Selly: with her."

### 9 · 2:32–2:50 · Why it holds up

**On screen:** three full-screen cards, one per sentence: "0% commission", "Paid straight into the seller's
wallet", "No bitcoin? Orange Money or Wave" (the guide). Then the Pricing page.
**Voice-over:**

> "Selly never touches the sales money and takes no commission. Sellers pay a fixed subscription, in sats.
> And for customers without bitcoin, a guide shows how to get some with Orange Money or Wave."

### 10 · 2:50–3:00 · The end

**On screen:** back to Sokhna's phone, then the Selly logo on paper, the mascot waving, the site's address.
**Voice-over:**

> "Your shop. Your assistant. Your money. Selly."

## Notes for the voice-over

- Record each shot as its own take, with a breath of silence before and after: the scenes are cut to the takes.
- Keep the second of silence at 1:59: the check mark is the moment people must remember.
- "Nanga def" (NAHN-gah def) and "Jërëjëf" (jeh-reh-JEFF) are in the chat on screen; the voice need not say them.
- Deliver as WAV or high-quality MP3, one file per shot (`01-hook.wav` … `10-end.wav`) or one file with the shots
  in order.
